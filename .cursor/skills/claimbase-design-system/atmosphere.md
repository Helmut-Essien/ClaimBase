# ClaimBase atmosphere

The sign-in brand panel is the only place that uses the purple wash. Claim tables, the rate matrix, and the sign-in form stay flat so the fields scan cleanly.

## Sign-in gradient

The brand panel runs from `#512BD4` (top left) to `#2B0B98` (bottom right). It holds the wordmark and the product sentence. The form sits on the page canvas beside it from `lg`, and below a short band on a phone.

## Leave off

- Rate cells, claim lines, session cards, and PDF-preview UI
- Grain, dot mesh, or a second product palette
- Dark mode as the default
